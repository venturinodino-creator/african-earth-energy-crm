Built around the sales conversation

Not a generic CRM with energy fields bolted on. Every screen exists because a rep needs it at a specific point in an offtaker conversation.

Fit scoring

Every offtaker gets a score out of 100 from load size, load factor, tariff headroom, wheeling feasibility and distance to the nearest AEE site. Sort by it and the call list writes itself.

Savings calculator

Model a wheeled PPA against the buyer's current tariff over 20 years, with escalation assumptions you can change live on the call. Copy the summary straight into an email.

PPA pipeline board

Seven stages from Identified to Signed. Drag a card, the probability updates and the move is written to the activity log automatically.

Capacity allocation

See how much of each site is already spoken for. Oversubscription is flagged when you create the opportunity, not after the term sheet goes out.

Load map

Offtaker load plotted against generation sites. Circle size is annual consumption, so the big prizes near a site are visible at a glance.

Playbook

Cold emails, a discovery script, objection handling and a qualification checklist — with the offtaker's own numbers filled in before you copy.

How a rep works an account

Four steps, start to signature.

1

Pick from the call list

The dashboard ranks untouched offtakers by fit score and tells you why each one scores where it does.

2

Open the record

Load profile, supply authority, wheeling feasibility, nearest site, the people, and an indicative saving — all on one screen.

3

Reach out and log it

Copy a pre-filled template, make the call, log what happened. The status moves off Prospect on its own.

4

Build the opportunity

Create a PPA opportunity against a specific site, model the saving, and drag it through the board as it progresses.

Start with the call list

Twenty offtakers are already loaded with estimated load profiles and tariffs. Verify the numbers as you go, add your own accounts, and export the list whenever you need to share it.
